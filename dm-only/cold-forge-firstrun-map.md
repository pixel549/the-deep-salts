# Cold Forge — First Run Instance Map (Session 9)
*DM reference only. Ephemeral — this layout dies with this instance.*

## Instance Identity
- **Recipe:** The Cold Forge
- **Flavor tag:** Combat (establishing enemy palette + biome identity)
- **Biome constant:** dry industrial heat, metal dust, amber light from cooling iron, no water anywhere. The forge went cold decades ago but the heat never fully left — it lives in the walls, the grating, the air itself. Everything is iron, brick, and soot.
- **Insight tier (Lloyd, 14/T5):** he reads the forge's history in its surfaces — tool marks that tell you who worked here and how long ago, heat stains that map the forge's slow death, the difference between iron that cooled naturally and iron that was *made* to stop.
- **Clean chalice flavor:** a stone quench trough, dry, with a chalice sitting in the basin where the water used to be.
- **Boss:** Foundry Marshal — guaranteed spawn (first run, never killed).

## Wake-In
**Cooling Gallery (A1)** — long, low corridor lined with iron racks. Once held cooling ingots; now empty, racks buckled. Heat radiates from the floor grating. Amber light from somewhere deeper. Two exits: forward (→A2), left side passage (→B1).

No enemies at wake. Orientation beat — let Lloyd look around, get his bearings. Maria comments on the heat.

## Main Path

**A2 — Slag Floor**
Open industrial space, maybe 15m across. Floor is uneven — cooled slag, poured and left. Three Cinderbound Attendants (mook, 220 HP each) shuffle through the space in loose patrol. They drift toward cold spots (Lloyd and Maria are cooler than the ambient). Two exits: far end (→A3), raised walkway right (→C1).

Loot: 8 White Salts in a cracked mould near the far exit.

**A3 — Chain Hoist Bay**
Vertical space — ceiling goes up three storeys, heavy chain hoists hanging from iron beams. One Anvil-Bound Apprentice (elite, 760 HP) blocks the main path, dragging its anvil in slow circles. A Fuse-Throated Crier (280 HP) stands on a raised platform 4m up — calls down Burning, can't be reached in melee without climbing (1 Full Action to climb, exposed).

Exits: forward (→A4), back (→A2), narrow gap under the hoist mechanism (→B2, hard to spot — Assess or deliberate search, DC 12).

Loot: Scour ×2 on the platform near the Crier. 12 White Salts (Apprentice drop + loose).

**A4 — Annealing Room**
Smaller, enclosed. Brick-lined ovens along both walls, doors hanging open. The heat here is the worst so far — ambient Burning: 1 stack/3 rounds spent in the room (environmental, not an enemy). A second Anvil-Bound Apprentice patrols slowly.

Exit: heavy iron door, warm to touch (→A5).

Loot: 15 White Salts in a sealed tool chest (no check to open, just time — 1 Full Action).

**A5 — The Casting Hall (Boss Room)**
The main forge floor. Massive space — 20m long, 12m wide, vaulted brick ceiling. The central casting pit is cold but stained with decades of poured metal. Rails run along the floor (don't touch the rails — the woman's note). The rails are hot — stepping on one = 1 Burning stack, immediate, no save.

**The Foundry Marshal** stands at the far end, motionless, facing the cold pit. Doesn't aggro until Lloyd enters the hall past the threshold (about 3m in) OR Maria casts into the room. Plenty of space to Assess from the doorway first.

Marshal stats: 1400 HP, 3m move. Marshal's Cross (70 raw + 1 Burning, 1.8s ±0.20s, Tier 2). Foundry Reckoning chain (3 windows: 0.90s/1.40s/1.90s each ±0.15s — all three parried = Tier 1 Open on Furnace Door, sever 300 at ×1.75 = 171 raw needed).

Two Cinderbound Attendants flank the pit — they activate when the Marshal does. Slow, manageable, but their Burning stacks compound with the Marshal's.

Exit beyond the Marshal: collapsed passage (→impassable this run). Side alcove visible behind the casting pit (→D1, accessible after or during the fight).

## Side Path B (optional)

**B1 — Tool Crib**
Small storage room off the Cooling Gallery. Locked cage (DC 14 Skill check to pick, or just break it — 1 Full Action, makes noise). Inside: **The Coil-Spring Gauntlet** (Weapon 24, Trick Skill↔Strength, Insight Tier 2 gate for full extension — Lloyd meets this easily at Insight 14).

One Cinderbound Attendant inside the cage with the weapon, dormant until the cage opens.

Loot: Gauntlet + 5 White Salts.

**B2 — Bellows Chamber (hidden)**
Accessed via narrow gap under the hoist in A3. Low-ceilinged, the old bellows mechanism — massive leather lungs, cracked and still. Quiet. No enemies.

This is where the clean chalice is. **A dry stone quench trough** against the far wall, a brass chalice sitting in the empty basin. The chalice is warm.

Loot: 10 White Salts in a canvas sack hanging from a bellows handle. A note tucked into the sack, different hand from the wall inscription upstairs — older, shakier: *"The Marshal doesn't chase. It waits. It always waits."*

## Side Path C (optional)

**C1 — Rail Spur Walkway**
Raised metal walkway overlooking the Slag Floor (A2). The hot rails from A5 extend here too — same rule, stepping on them = 1 Burning. Narrow (2m wide), no cover.

One Frost-Iron Smith (brute, 1600 HP, 90 raw + Burning) stands at the far end of the walkway, blocking access to C2. Fighting on the narrow walkway is dangerous — no room to kite, but the Smith's 4m move means Lloyd can potentially bait and retreat back to A2's open space.

**C2 — Coal Store**
Beyond the Smith. Dark, cool (the only cool room in the instance). Coal dust everywhere. No enemies.

Loot: **The Foundry Wedge** (Weapon 5, Strength/B, best raw sever weapon in the game — but it's a Strength weapon, not ideal for Lloyd's build). 20 White Salts loose in a broken crate.

Also here: a second note in the same steady hand as the wall inscription downstairs. *"Second run — the Foundry floor. Don't touch the rails. The Marshal opens after the third chain. Every time. Patient."* This is the missing second entry from the woman's run log.

## Side Path D

**D1 — Foreman's Alcove**
Small room behind the casting pit in A5. Accessible during or after the Marshal fight. Contains a desk, a ledger, a chair. No enemies.

The ledger is a work log — shift assignments, output tallies, disciplinary notes. Written in the Hydro's standard administrative shorthand. One entry near the back, circled in a different ink: a name. **"Nell."** Different context than the Choir Deep — this is a work assignment, not a rota. Nell pulled shifts in the Cold Forge too.

Loot: 8 White Salts in the desk drawer.

## Pacing Notes
- First run = orientation. The woman's note said it. Let Lloyd find his feet.
- The clean chalice (B2) is hidden but not brutally so — reward exploration.
- The Marshal is the set piece but NOT mandatory this run if Lloyd wants to anchor out first and come back prepared.
- The Frost-Iron Smith on the walkway is a genuine wall — 1600 HP, 90 raw hits, narrow space. Lloyd can skip it entirely (C1/C2 are optional). The Foundry Wedge behind it is a Strength weapon anyway.
- Burning is the recipe's signature pressure — it compounds. Lloyd has Scour ×1 (hub kit) and can find Scour ×2 more in A3. Maria has no Burning management.
- The hot rails are a hazard, not a puzzle. Just don't step on them.
- Nell's name in D1 is a nudge, not a reveal.
