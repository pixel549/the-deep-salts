## THROUGHLINE

The linen contagion. Came in on a mismatched external delivery — 14 bundles received, 13 dispatched, signed T.T. Transmits by handling, not proximity. Does not kill: strips personhood and leaves the task. Victims become aprons — the garment of their role, hanging at working height, performing their last job indefinitely. Slope, not a switch: world → reasons → questions → nothing. Rate of decline scales with volume of infected linen handled.

**RESOLVED session 11.** All 48 aprons released by burning the building's entire linen stock in one pile on the wash floor. This is the instance's central puzzle and it is done. Do not re-litigate it.

**What is NOT resolved:** where the fourteenth bundle came from. The supplier is in the village. This is the run's remaining spine.

## MAP 2 — THE CELLAR *(immediate next beat)*

The crossed circle is the mark Ada said was retired before she started and nobody would explain. Tolliver wore it. **It is the mark of the room that handles what cannot be washed.**

- **Race chamber.** The water under the building, running properly — the only honest water in the instance. Pre-hospital; the hospital was built on top of something.
- **The retired room.** Where the crossed-circle linen went: burned, not washed. A furnace, cold twelve years.
- **The fourteenth bundle.** Placed here, unburned, still sealed. Tolliver couldn't bring himself to destroy the evidence of what he signed for. **This is the anchor candidate** — see below.
- **Hazard:** handling it is the transmission vector. Lloyd touching it is a real risk. Maria touching it is worse (she's the one who'd fold it).
- **Loot:** 1 stash (~20–25 salts), Purging Salts ×2 (thematically correct — the room for what can't be cleaned).

## MAP 3 — THE VILLAGE (2mi downhill)

Small. Do not build a continent. Six locations max.

- **Mill Row** — Mrs A. Cobbett at no. 4. Tolliver's unsent letter is to her. Sister? Widow? Leave open; decide when Jake gets there.
- **The laundry contractor** — the external supplier from the kitchen ledger. Where 14 bundles left from. **The source.**
- **The church on the hill** — the light they saw from the forecourt. One light went out while they watched: something is awake down there.
- **The inn** — rare in-run NPC slot already spent on Ada; a second NPC here needs justification, not a reroll.
- **Boundary behaviour:** the village should be *populated but wrong* — the instance has never had an exterior before and shouldn't suddenly become a coherent world. People, but the same person's face used twice. A road that goes downhill both ways.

**Design guard:** Maria has staked a position that the instances are real places. Lloyd has staked the opposite. **Do not resolve this.** Give evidence to both sides and let it stay live.
