## THE CENTRAL PUZZLE — the draught circuit

The Works is a heat-distribution system with **four dampers** (D1–D4) and a **control frame** in the Damper Loft (R4). At any moment, draught runs through exactly **two** of four routes; the other two are cold and still.

**A flue carrying draught is impassable** — not lethal instantly, but 1 Burning per round inside it, and Burning is the biome's signature pressure. **A cold flue is a corridor.** So the puzzle is: *route the heat away from where you want to walk, and onto where you want it to go.*

Second-order use, and the good one — **the heat is a weapon and a key**:
- Route draught into the **Slag Chute (R6)** and the seized iron door at its foot expands and cracks its own frame → opens the way to R7.
- Route draught *onto an enemy position* and it burns for you. The Kiln-Moths in R3 will follow heat anywhere; feeding a flue draws them off a route entirely.
- Route draught *away from* the Draught (R5) and it starves — see R5.

**Damper states at wake-in:** D1 open, D2 open, D3 shut, D4 shut. Live draught: **Route A (R2→R3→R5)** and **Route C (R3→R8)**. Cold: the Slag Chute and the Loft return.

**Damper controls:** D1 and D2 are on the floor at their flue mouths, hand-worked, 1 Full Action each, no check. **D3 and D4 are chain-worked from the control frame in R4 only** — which is the room the whole layout is pointing at. He does not need to be told this; the chains are visible running up from D3/D4 into the ceiling.

**DM discipline:** do not pre-solve. Describe warm brick, moving air, chain runs, and a needle gauge. Hazards named only once he identifies them (protocols §3.5). If he never engages the circuit, the run still works — every route has a fight-through option, it just costs Burning and Scour.

**Live damper state:** *(update as he works it)* — D1 SHUT · D2 SHUT (Lloyd found the matching hand-worked lever at the R8 mouth, same setup as D1, shut it before Maria followed him in) · D3 shut · D4 shut. **All four dampers are now shut.** Route A and Route C both cold throughout — every mouth in R3 is dead quiet now, moths have nothing left to orient on. **Not yet revealed to the player: per the R9 Bellows Root entry, the draught isn't generated by the dampers, it's generated by the Root itself — shutting every valve doesn't mean the building's stopped breathing, it just means the air has nowhere it's currently being routed to. Hold this thread for the Bellows Chamber reveal; don't spoil it now.**
