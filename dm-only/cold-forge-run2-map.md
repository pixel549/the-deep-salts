## Biome constant (inherited, run 1)

Dry industrial heat. Metal dust. Amber light off iron that never fully cooled. **No water anywhere.** Iron, brick, soot. The forge went cold decades ago; the heat lives in the walls.

**Sub-locale layer on top:** everything here is *between* the walls rather than inside a room. Brick flues wide enough to walk. Iron dampers on chains. Grated catwalks over ducting. The recurring wrong note: **the draught still runs.** Every fire in this building has been out for decades and the air is still moving, steadily, in one direction, and it is warm.

## THE CENTRAL PUZZLE — the draught circuit

The Works is a heat-distribution system with **four dampers** (D1–D4) and a **control frame** in the Damper Loft (R4). At any moment, draught runs through exactly **two** of four routes; the other two are cold and still.

**A flue carrying draught is impassable** — not lethal instantly, but 1 Burning per round inside it, and Burning is the biome's signature pressure. **A cold flue is a corridor.** So the puzzle is: *route the heat away from where you want to walk, and onto where you want it to go.*

Second-order use, and the good one — **the heat is a weapon and a key**:
- Route draught into the **Slag Chute (R6)** and the seized iron door at its foot expands and cracks its own frame → opens the way to R7.
- Route draught *onto an enemy position* and it burns for you. The Kiln-Moths in R3 will follow heat anywhere; feeding a flue draws them off a route entirely.
- Route draught *away from* the Draught (R5) and it starves — see R5.

**Damper states at wake-in:** D1 open, D2 open, D3 shut, D4 shut. Live draught: **Route A (R2→R3→R5)** and **Route C (R3→R8)**. Cold: the Slag Chute and the Loft return.

**Damper controls:** D1 and D2 are on the floor at their flue mouths, hand-worked, 1 Full Action each, no check. **D3 and D4 are chain-worked from the control frame in R4 only** — which is the room the whole layout is pointing at. He does not need to be told this; the chains are visible running up from D3/D4 into the ceiling.

**DM discipline:** do not pre-solve. Describe warm brick, moving air, chain runs, and a needle gauge. Hazards named only once he identifies them (protocols §3.5). If he never engages the circuit, the run still works — every route has a fight-through option, it just costs Burning and Scour.

**Live damper state:** *(update as he works it)* — D1 open · D2 open · D3 shut · D4 shut.

## Rooms

### R1 — The Ash Landing *(wake-in)*
A brick half-landing at the foot of a flue wide enough to stand in. Ankle-deep soft grey ash, undisturbed except where the air has drawn it into ripples all pointing the same way — **the tell for the whole instance, sitting in the first room, unremarked.** Warm underfoot. Iron rungs up one wall. Two ways on: the flue mouth ahead (→R2), and a low grated crawl to the side, cold air coming out of it (→R6, the Slag Chute head).

No enemies. Orientation beat. Let him look. Maria: the heat doesn't bother her the way the cold of the Choir did — she says so if prompted, and doesn't elaborate.

**Buried find:** something is half-buried in the ash against the far wall, only the corner showing and it reads as brick until touched — **a japanned tin lunch pail**, cold, sealed. Inside: 14 White Salts, a Scour, and a folded paper that is *not* the third note (it's a shift roster fragment, no names legible). Deliberately a near-miss on the woman's thread — establishes the object type without paying it out.

- **Loot:** 14 salts, Scour ×1, roster fragment (flavour).
- **State:** unvisited.

### R2 — The Feed Flue *(live draught at wake-in)*
A walkable brick flue, 2m wide, running uphill. Currently carrying draught: **1 Burning per round inside.** ~4 rounds to cross at walking pace, or he can shut **D1** at the mouth (visible, hand-worked, 1 Full Action) and walk it cold. First real statement of the mechanic; make the wrong-way version survivable, not punishing.

Walls are lined with soot except for a chest-height band where decades of hands have worn it clean — people walked this every day.

- **Loot:** none. This room is tuition.
- **State:** unvisited.

### R3 — The Crossing Chamber *(hub room — Kiln-Moths)*
Where all four flues meet. A brick vault, 12m across, four dark mouths at the compass points, iron catwalk ringing it 3m up with a gap where a section has fallen. **Ceiling height is the fight.**

**Encounter: two Kiln-Moths (Monster 43, 500 HP each).** New archetype to Lloyd — **+1 Insight each on first sighting, so +1 total for the archetype** (§17: first sighting of a *new archetype*, not per body). They orient on heat, not people, so at wake-in they are circling the two live flue mouths, not the door. **They do not aggro until something in the room is the hottest thing in it** — i.e. until Lloyd walks out of a live flue carrying Burning, or Maria opens with Purging Flame.

This is the instance's thesis stated in a fight: *he can start this encounter on his terms or hand it to them.*

Wing sever (200) is well inside his numbers — Heavy at ESV 20 is 59 base, ×1.25 wing = 73, and a visceral or a good Powder Charge doubles that. Grounding one is achievable in two clean hits. Let that land as a reward.

**Second-order:** if he opens D3 or D4 later and routes draught through a *different* mouth, any surviving Moth relocates to it. They can be parked.

- **Loot:** 11 White Salts scattered in the ash under the catwalk gap (where someone fell). On the catwalk itself, wedged where the section broke: **Censer Incense ×1**.
- **Insight:** +1 (Kiln-Moth first sighting).
- **State:** unvisited. Moths alive: 2.

### R4 — The Damper Loft *(the puzzle room)*
Up the rungs off R3, a low timber-and-iron loft over the crossing. Four chain runs come up through the floor to a control frame — four levers, each with a brass tag, and a **gauge with a needle** on the wall above showing total draught pressure. Two levers sit forward, two back.

**Working a lever is 1 Fast Action.** The frame tells him nothing about *which* route each lever serves — the tags are worn. He has to correlate: pull a lever, hear where the air moves, or watch the ash. That correlation *is* the puzzle. Assess or a Skill check (DC 13) shortcuts one tag; the rest is observation.

**Encounter: The Regulator (Monster 103, 620 HP) — NEW, first appearance, first Rite in the campaign.** It is up here doing its rounds, walking the frame, checking the gauge. **It does not attack. It will not attack unless something in the room is burning.** If Lloyd comes up cold and Maria holds her fire, he can share this room with it indefinitely, work the levers beside it, and leave — and that is the *intended clean solve*, not a missed fight.

The moment Maria opens with Purging Flame (her default, and she will if told to engage), the room becomes a fight and the Regulator goes for **her**, not him. Let that read clearly and do not soften it. **+1 Insight on first sighting** (Rite is a new archetype *and* a new monster).

**Do not state the rule.** Assess 2+ gives the directional hint in the statblock and nothing more.

- **Loot:** on the frame, hung on a nail like it belongs there — **the Kiln Fork** (Weapon 9, verified: Heavy, Strength/C, two-handed, Base 28; at Lloyd's ESV 5 that is Light 32 / **Heavy 61**). Passive: **any Heavy hit applies 1 Burning**, +25% raw vs Discombobulated. **This is a cursed prize in this specific instance and that is the joke** — the one weapon he finds here feeds the Regulator, the Draught, the Smith's Quench and the Root's Bank. Do not warn him. Assess on any of those enemies will. 9 White Salts in a tin on the sill.
- **Insight:** +1 (Regulator first sighting).
- **State:** unvisited. Regulator alive, unprovoked.

### R5 — The Draught's Run *(elite, optional, avoidable)*
The long return flue, carrying live draught at wake-in. **The Draught (Monster 62, 700 HP, elite)** stands in it — which is *why* the draught runs, or so it appears. It is drawn toward heat and it is standing in the warmest place available.

**The trick:** shut the route (D1 + D3 work) and the flue goes cold. The Draught **does not die** and is not weakened mechanically — but it leaves, following heat to wherever he has just routed it. He can move this enemy without fighting it. If he routes it into R8 with the Frost-Iron Smith, they are in the same corridor and the Smith's Burning output feeds it — genuinely funny, entirely legal, and a real solve.

If he fights it: it eats Burning, so Maria's Purging Flame is again the wrong tool, and the Choir Needle is exactly the right one. Double-mouth sever kills it outright regardless of HP.

- **Loot:** 18 White Salts in a soot-caked canvas bag pinned under a fallen damper plate.
- **State:** unvisited. Draught present, live route.

### R6 — The Slag Chute *(cold at wake-in — the seized door)*
A steep cold chute of firebrick, descending. At its foot, an **iron door seized solid in its frame** — decades of expansion and contraction have welded it shut. Strength check to force is DC 22 (Extreme; Lloyd at −1 is not doing this, and he should be allowed to try and fail cheaply).

**The intended answer is the puzzle:** route draught down the chute (D2 shut, D4 open) and leave it running two rounds. The frame expands, the weld cracks, the door swings. **Waiting in the chute while it heats costs him** — he must clear the chute and come back, which means committing to the plan before he sees it work.

Beyond: **R7.**

- **Loot:** none in the chute itself.
- **State:** unvisited. Door seized. Draught: cold.

### R7 — The Regulator's Rest *(the instance quest payoff)* — **INSTANCE QUEST**
A small brick chamber behind the seized door, cold, airtight, undisturbed since it was sealed. This is the run's quiet room and its narrative point.

**The unknown woman is here.** Not a corpse in the theatrical sense — she is sitting against the wall with her legs out, her coat still buttoned, and she has been dry and cold and sealed in for a long time. Nothing has touched her. The door seized behind her; there is no mechanism on this side, and there never was.

She is not an enemy. She does not move. **Do not animate her, do not have her speak, and do not turn this into a fight.** The reward here is an answer, and the answer is mundane and complete: she found the best room in the instance, and the building shut a door.

**Her third note is in her hand, written and never delivered** — the blank third entry in Lloyd's set is blank because *this* is the third entry. It is short. It says the chalice is closer to the entrance than anyone thinks, that she went back for the Wedge, and that the door was open when she came through. Then, in a different pressure of pen, at the bottom, added later and much shakier: a line about how long she has been counting the draught, and that it changed direction once, and that she thinks somebody adjusted it.

**Instance Quest — "The Third Run."** Completed on reading the note. Converts to a persistent reward per §18: **her Forge-Ledger Key** (a brass tag on a cord, hers, worn) — persistent item, no mechanical effect established, and it is a physical link to the Cold Forge recipe itself. Also releases the *woman* thread from "unresolved" to "resolved, identity still unknown" — she never signs her name, and Lloyd will not learn it here. Rotation-wise this pays out a thread sitting at 2+ sessions **without** touching Nell (rotation 0 — leave alone).

**Her gear, on and around her:** her coat (nothing), a spent lamp, **Amber Forge-Salts ×2**, 40 White Salts in a purse she never got to bank, and **the Foundry Wedge** — the same weapon that was behind the Frost-Iron Smith on run 1, which she went back for and got. (Weapon 5, verified: Heavy, Strength/B, two-handed, Base 30; at Lloyd's ESV 5 that is Light 35 / **Heavy 67**. Marginally above his Knuckles' 59 Heavy, two-handed, no Powder Charge, no Precision passive — a sidegrade at best for a Skill build.) Still the wrong weapon for Lloyd, and that is fine; the point is that she *got it* and it did not save her. If he takes it, he takes it.

**Do not summarise this room as loot.** Describe a sealed cold room with a woman in it and let him search.

- **Loot:** third note (quest), Forge-Ledger Key (persistent), Amber Forge-Salts ×2, 40 salts, Foundry Wedge.
- **State:** unvisited, sealed.

### R8 — The Long Return *(the skipped wall — optional)*
A straight 30m flue on Route C, live draught at wake-in. **The Frost-Iron Smith (Monster 30, 1600 HP)** stands in it, exactly the fight he walked away from on run 1. Deliberate callback; he has since gained a level, a band of Strength, Tier 5, and a precision sidearm.

Narrow (2.5m) — no room to circle, and the flue is *live*, so fighting here is fighting under 1 Burning per round. **Shut the route first and this becomes a normal fight.** That is the whole design: the wall he ran from is beatable now if he uses the room instead of his stopwatch.

Leg sever (200 stagger, ×0.9) neutralises the approach and Assess 2+ says so.

Beyond it: **R9.**

- **Loot:** 20 White Salts on the Smith. Nothing else in the flue.
- **State:** unvisited. Smith present, live route.

### R9 — The Bellows Chamber *(set piece, optional, the answer to the question)*
Past the Smith, the flue opens into a brick drum 15m across with a domed roof. **The Bellows Root (Monster 90, 1800 HP, Snare elite)** fills the middle of it, four leather arms off a central knot, each arm having scorched its own quadrant of the floor black.

**This is why the draught still runs.** The forge is cold; the building is breathing because *this* is breathing. Lloyd should be able to work that out from the room without being told — the knot inflates on a slow schedule and every damper in the Works twitches on the same beat.

**Consequence if killed:** the draught stops entirely. Every flue goes cold within a few rounds, every route opens, the Kiln-Moths lose their orientation and scatter, the Regulator has nothing left to regulate and simply stops. Killing it solves the whole level — *after* the level no longer needs solving. **That irony is the intended payoff and should not be spoiled in advance.** If he kills it early having guessed, let him have it; that is a brilliant read and it should work.

**Do not burn it** (Bank, +15 raw per stack cumulative). Maria's Purging Flame is wrong here for the third time this instance, and by now that should be a pattern he has noticed rather than a gotcha.

- **Loot:** 42 salts (Root). In the drum's lip, above the scorch line where nothing has ever reached: **a coil of brass chain and a damper tag stamped with a number that matches nothing in the Works.** Flavour, unresolved, no payout — do not invent one.
- **State:** unvisited. Root alive, breathing.

## THE ANCHOR — placed, confirmed

**The clean chalice is in R1's side alcove, off the Ash Landing, six metres from where he wakes.**

A brick niche behind the iron rungs, at head height, easy to walk past and *not* hidden by a check — it is simply behind him at wake-in and he will be looking forward. Inside the niche: a **dry firebrick mould with a brass chalice seated in it**, warm to the touch like everything else here.

The woman's third note (R7) says the chalice is closer to the entrance than anyone thinks. **She is describing this.** If he reads the note before finding the chalice, he gets a genuine "…it was behind me" beat. If he finds it first, the note confirms he was right. Both are good.

**Anchor status: PLACED. Verified before wake-in.** Do not narrate the wake-in without this line being true. (Run 5 failure, session 11.)
