## Instance Map — The Choir Deep (rerolled fresh)

### R1 — The Narthex (wake-in, safe)
- Narrow stone antechamber. Cracked baptismal font, water-stained tiles. Two exits: left (low arch) and right (stairs down).
- **Enemies:** none. **Loot:** none visible.
- Ceiling fresco — at Tier 4, Lloyd reads devotional script running along the border. At Tier 5 he'd have caught the second layer underneath. Don't mention what he can't see.

### R2 — The Undercroft (from R1 left)
- Low barrel-vaulted passage. Standing water, stone shelving along both walls, some collapsed.
- **Enemies:** 2× Chapel Penitent (300 HP each). Kneeling in the water, rise when approached within 5m.
- **Loot:** 12 White Salts in a collapsed shelf (investigation — describe scattered objects, don't announce). Censer Incense ×1 tucked behind a broken column base (same — visible if looked for, not called out).
- Exit: forward to R4 (The Processional).

### R3 — The Choir Pit (from R1 right, stairs down)
- Sunken oval rehearsal space. Tiered stone benches descending to a central drain. Water knee-deep at the bottom.
- **Enemies:** 1× Drowned Bellkeeper (280 HP, upper tier) + 2× Waterlogged Guest (200 HP each, wading below). Bellkeeper tolls from elevation; Guests shamble toward Lloyd if he descends.
- **Loot:** After combat, if the drain grate is investigated: **Choir Needle** (Weapon 14, Skill/D, Base 22) wedged in the ironwork. Don't announce — describe the grate, the glint of metal.
- Exit: forward to R4 (The Processional).

### R4 — The Processional (from R2 or R3)
- Long narrow corridor. Water flowing in a carved floor-channel. Walls carved with hundreds of names, too small to read while walking.
- **Enemies:** 1× Salt-Eaten Custodian (650 HP). Patrolling slowly. Corridor is ~3m wide — limited kiting, can't easily circle. The "narrow fight" the dm-plan flagged for this session.
- **Loot:** none dropped.
- **Lore (names on wall, requires stopping + Insight Read):** Nell's name. Scratched deeper than the others, with a date that falls within the gap year from the Choir rota. Third cross-recipe Nell context. At Tier 4: Lloyd reads the name and date clearly. At Tier 5 (if he still had it): he'd have caught that the scratching was done by a different hand than the carved names — someone added her later.
- Exit: forward to R5 (side door) or straight to R6 (through an iron gate).

### R5 — The Reliquary (from R4, side door)
- Small, cold room. Glass-fronted cabinets, mostly empty/smashed. Wax and old metal smell.
- **Enemies:** none. Quiet room.
- **Loot:** Locked cabinet (Strength DC 12, or smash it — no check, but noise draws attention from R6 if the Marble Attendant is still alive — it begins advancing toward R5 after 2 turns of noise). Inside: **Bent Tuning Key** (Item 33 — Precision Strike windows treated as one Insight tier higher than actual). VERY relevant since Lloyd dropped from Tier 5 to Tier 4.
- **Lore:** A ledger — choir attendance records, formal, institutional. Maria recognizes the format immediately. She goes quiet, turns pages carefully, then closes it and says: "We kept ours the same way." First direct admission her order's record-keeping matches the Choir Deep's. Don't push further — let Jake pull on this if he wants.
- Exit: back to R4.

### R6 — The Chancel (from R4, through iron gate)
- High-ceilinged, open. Raised stone performance platform. Water cascading from a ceiling crack.
- **Hazard:** wet stone = rough terrain, +1m movement cost.
- **Enemies:** 1× Marble Attendant (800 HP). Standing motionless on the platform. 80 raw hits. Tier 2 parry window. Long recovery — the real threat is trading hits, not tempo.
- **Loot:** Behind the platform, half-submerged, a sealed reliquary box. Requires investigation. Contains: **Widow's Veil** (Item 37 — removes wielder from Influence Overload redirect pool, rest of encounter). Rare, single-use. Don't describe as an item — describe the box, its seal, the weight.
- Exit: up a narrow stair to R7.

### R7 — The Bell Tower Base (from R6, narrow stair)
- Bottom of a collapsed bell tower. Spiral staircase, partial. One bell hanging askew above. Rope dangling.
- **Enemies:** 1× Bathhouse Flailer (700 HP). On the lower staircase. Tight space — erratic movement more dangerous here. The Frenzied Whirl chain (3 sequential windows: 0.80s/1.30s/1.80s) in a confined area where repositioning is limited.
- **Loot:** After combat. The bell rope — if pulled, the bell falls (dodge: trivial). Inside the bell: 25 White Salts + a torn page from a church register. Entry reads: "Sr. M——, seconded to Stillwell, [date]." Date is within the same range as Nell's gap year and the rota gap. Maria sees Lloyd reading it. She doesn't ask what it says. She already knows.
- Exit: through a low archway to R8.

### R8 — The Apse (boss room — EMPTY this run)
- Vast semicircular space. Mosaic ceiling — at Tier 4, Lloyd reads the figurative elements (pilgrims, water, a descent) but the inscriptions between the panels blur. At Tier 5 he'd have read the full liturgy.
- **No Singer.** The channels in the floor run with water but nothing is at the source. Scorch marks where it was rooted last time. Dried residue in a ring on the stone — the congregation's outline.
- **Enemies:** none.
- **Loot:** 15 White Salts in a basin near the exit chalice. A **Sigil Lash** (Weapon 20, Resolve/D, Base 20) leaning against the wall where the Singer stood — as if left behind, or placed there. Resolve weapon — not Lloyd's build, but could be Maria's if equipped. Don't announce — describe the object.
- **Exit:** the chalice. Banking out.
- **Narrative beat:** The mosaic ceiling depicts a procession descending into water. The last figure has no face — just an opening. At Tier 4, Lloyd reads this as the Singer's origin. At Tier 5, he'd have read the inscription beneath it as a name. He can't now. He knows there's something there he used to be able to see. First real consequence of trading Insight.
