## What's ripe

**The flooded gallery.** Not visited since session 5. Four sessions ago. It's the least developed recipe and the most mysterious. Could be where Nell appears in a fourth context — or where the pattern breaks. Lloyd's at Insight 8 now; he'll see this place differently than he did at Insight 2-3 when he first explored it. Some things might be clearer (Tier 4 > Tier 1), some might be different. Fresh reroll.

**The Frost-Iron Smith.** Still blocking the rail spur walkway in the Cold Forge. Scouted, not fought. Narrow space, 90 raw hits. The dm-plan flagged this as a fight "where Lloyd's strengths don't fully apply" — the Processional Custodian served that purpose this session, but the Smith is still out there.

**Maria's thread — accelerating.** Three admissions this session: (1) "We kept ours the same way" about the ledger format. (2) Defended the Church genuinely when Lloyd questioned it — she doesn't know. (3) Saw the torn register page "Sr. M——" and said nothing. The linen strip, the ward-marks on the Sigil Lash, the matching dates. She's sitting on a pile of connections she hasn't assembled yet. Don't push — let Lloyd pull. But seed one more thing next session that makes it harder for her to not see.

**Nell.** Three contexts now. The pattern is: she appears in institutional records, always in the gap year, always added by a different hand. Someone kept writing her in. The question Lloyd asked — "who kept writing her in?" — is the right question. Needs eventual payoff. Don't rush it.

**The mosaic inscription.** Lloyd knows there's something there he can't read anymore. This is the cost of the Vendor trade made tangible. If Insight climbs back above 10 (Tier 5), he can read it. That's a genuine carrot — but Insight is scarce and non-renewable from kills. The Vendor would cost more mutations. This tension is by design.

**The Choir Needle.** Lloyd has it but hasn't used it. It's a precision sidearm — self-initiated called shots eased one band. This shines against stationary or staggered targets where he doesn't have a parry opening. Worth reminding him it exists when the situation fits.

## Combat design notes for next session

- The Custodian fight in the narrow Processional was the first real test of the new combat flow rules. It worked — tight space forced commitment. Do more of this.
- Multi-enemy simultaneous action is now codified. Next session should include at least one fight with 3+ enemies where the "one parry per round" rule has real teeth — Lloyd can't just hold through everything.
- Lloyd's parry game is strong. He landed every stopwatch this session. Start seeding enemies with unparryable components (chip damage, auras) alongside parryable heavies to force trade decisions.
- Maria's Sigil Lash hasn't been tested in a real fight yet. Next session should include an encounter where Influence pressure is the threat and the Lash's −1 resistance debuff matters.

## Memory Vendor — status

Lloyd visited, bought two mutations, dropped from Insight 18 to 8. Vendor is invisible to him now (threshold 15). He'd need to earn 7 more Insight to see her again. At his current kill rate (most common enemies already discovered, +0 on repeats), that's a lot of new discoveries. The Vendor visit is a rare event, not a routine shop trip. That's correct.
